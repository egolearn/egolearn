## Scientific protocol

Retain validated ViT-S/16 (384/12/6), predictor192/6/6, 16 frames at prepared 5fps, 128px crops, BF16, seed0. Batch32 clips, streaming stride2 complete clips, FIFO32 clips; first fill30 clips then advance2; one update per advance. Chronological ordering follows existing SAYCam-S and natural child/date/part BabyView adapters. No clip crosses a recording or timestamp discontinuity (nonpositive/nonfinite/>0.3sec). Drop incomplete clip tails, record exact counts. Replay consists only of this rolling window; no reservoir or future clips. All complete clips enter the FIFO once. Tail advance of one clip still trains a full window; trailing frames which produce no clip must NOT cause another update. Drain trailing observations after the last training update and assert final source and emitted-clip counts.

IID shuffles the identical nonoverlapping clip inventory every epoch, drops incomplete batches, uses the SAME exact update budget U=ceil((N-B+S)/S). Exact dataset counts and resulting update totals are captured in private launch manifests. IID effective passes U*32/N ~16. Start all four runs from scratch, random checkpoint per run; same initialization within dataset/mode pairs. LR1e-4 (retain pilot), 5% step-based warmup then cosine, 100 minimum warmup steps, weight decay.04, constant EMA.998 in both modes. This is a controlled small-model adaptation, not Meta-scale reproduction. Step-based schedule must give exactly equal LR by update in both modes irrespective of observation progress. Keep raw stream progress for diagnostics. No grad accumulation or streaming prefetch.
